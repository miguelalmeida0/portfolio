## What Elite Animated Sites Do Differently

Elite animated sites design in time, not only in layout. Motion is a page system with roles, triggers, states, and section handoffs. A strong page has one or more signature motion moments, but those moments are supported by smaller layers: navigation state, menu reveals, image/media progression, typography rhythm, hover/press feedback, and scroll pacing.

The key difference from AI-generated motion is intent. Award-level motion is not a single drifting background or fade-up recipe. It makes the page feel authored: reel states, pinned sections, cinematic media reveals, kinetic text, and scroll-linked transformations create a recognizable sequence.

## Motion Roles

- **Page / section choreography:** controls the story arc across scroll.
- **Image / media choreography:** reveals, masks, crops, expands, or sequences visual material.
- **Typography choreography:** creates reading rhythm and hierarchy.
- **Navigation/menu choreography:** makes orientation and route state feel composed.
- **Micro-feedback:** gives buttons, cards, filters, menus, and CTAs physical response.
- **Signature motion moment:** the memorable motion event that makes the page feel specific.

## What Design OS Was Missing

- A benchmark layer specifically for motion.
- A local motion sequence capture workflow.
- A tool stack decision tree.
- A required storyboard/choreography artifact.
- A minimum layer count for advanced motion.
- Hard enforcement against "one animation equals advanced motion."
- Prompt templates that make agents plan time, not just layout.

## What Must Become Standard

When Migi asks for advanced, cinematic, artistic, Awwwards/Godly/Recent-level, scroll, or portfolio motion:

1. Name exact motion references and techniques.
2. Create `docs/design/motion-choreography-plan.md`.
3. Choose a tool stack deliberately.
4. Define at least five motion layers for advanced motion.
5. Map motion section-by-section.
6. Define reduced-motion behavior.
7. Capture screenshot sequence or document a blocker.
8. Run animation review.
9. Score against `evaluation/benchmarks/motion-cinematic.benchmark.md`.

## Risks Of Blind Copying

- Copying brand motion without matching product concept.
- Adding smooth-scroll libraries to productivity flows where speed matters.
- Using scroll hijacking that hurts accessibility.
- Animating important text before it is readable.
- Hiding content behind reel/menu states that users cannot control.
- Treating references as visual assets instead of motion mechanics.
