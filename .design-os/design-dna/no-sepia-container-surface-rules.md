# No Sepia Container Surfaces

The muddy sepia/espresso/umber brown-black archive wash is a critical blocker for Migi work.

This is not a palette preference and not a theme option. It is a repeated AI-generated UI tell.

## Hard Ban

Do not use this color family on:

- page backgrounds
- hero/stage backgrounds
- cards
- panels
- containers
- sidebars
- navigation bars
- framed media/object surfaces
- sticky/fixed chrome
- drawers, sheets, modals, and detail panels

## What Counts

Ban the surface if it reads as:

- muddy sepia
- espresso brown
- umber brown-black
- tobacco brown
- dirty bronze
- stale parchment
- dark olive-brown
- archive/nocturne brown
- old-coffee container surface

Common visual signs:

- near-black brown container instead of real black, ink, graphite, or colored dark
- cream text and brass/gold accents used to make the brown feel intentional
- rounded panel/card with a brown-black fill
- repeated brown surfaces across unrelated products
- product copy rationalizes the color as archive, premium, vintage, field guide, nocturne, brass, botanical, tobacco, parchment, cinematic, or command-center mood

## No Rationalizations

Do not justify this palette with:

- nocturne
- archive
- field guide
- botanical
- brass
- tobacco
- parchment
- vintage
- cinematic
- premium
- commander
- incident
- operational
- warm dark mode

## Required Replacement

Redesign the palette before continuing.

Use a distinct, authored color system such as:

- clean black and cream
- ink blue
- aubergine-black
- deep green-black
- mineral graphite
- cold moonlit blue
- oxidized red/black
- chrome blue/graphite
- domain-specific palette from approved references

The replacement must pass contrast review and preserve hierarchy without reverting to the banned brown family.

## Score Caps

- sepia/espresso/umber surface appears on page/stage/app background: max score 2
- sepia/espresso/umber surface appears on card/panel/container: max score 2
- sepia/espresso/umber surface appears on nav/sidebar/chrome: max score 2
- repeated sepia/espresso/umber palette across unrelated generated UIs: max score 2
- banned brown surface justified as premium/archive/nocturne/etc.: max score 2
- final handoff claims success while sepia container remains visible: max score 2
