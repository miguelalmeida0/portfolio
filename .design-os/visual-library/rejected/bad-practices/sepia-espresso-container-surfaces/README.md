# Sepia Espresso Container Surfaces

Muddy sepia/espresso/umber brown-black container surfaces are banned.

## What Failed

The Cascade Lab commander-note panel reused the same rejected brown-black surface family inside a large rounded container. Cream copy and gold accents did not make it authored; they made the AI-default palette more obvious.

## Why It Hurts

This surface family has appeared repeatedly across unrelated generated UIs. It makes the product feel stale, robotic, and generated, even when the layout itself is readable.

## Detection

- large brown-black panel/card/container
- gold/brass accents over dark brown
- cream text on muddy espresso background
- palette described as commander, archive, nocturne, premium, incident, or warm dark mode

## Fix

Remove the brown container surface and redesign the palette from product domain or approved references.

## Score Caps

- sepia/espresso/umber surface appears on card/panel/container: max score 2
- final handoff claims success while sepia container remains visible: max score 2
