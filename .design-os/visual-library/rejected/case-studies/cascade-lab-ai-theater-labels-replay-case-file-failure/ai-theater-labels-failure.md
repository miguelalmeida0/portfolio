# AI Theater Labels Failure

The chips `Narrative`, `Fictional incident`, and `SEV-2` read as generator-made product theater.

They do not organize user work, change scope, expose real data, or help the user decide what to do next. Repeating this kind of status chrome across pages creates an artificial "incident movie" layer on top of the product.

## Rule

Remove decorative narrative/status chips unless they are backed by real domain behavior and a documented task.

## Score Caps

- fake narrative/fictional incident chip: max score 4
- fake severity/status badge used as theater: max score 4
- repeated AI-theater chips across pages: max score 3
