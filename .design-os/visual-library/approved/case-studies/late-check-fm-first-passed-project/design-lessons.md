# Design Lessons

## Concept Strength

The Gen-Z hostel concept had enough specificity to carry a memorable interface:

- radio/broadcast naming
- hostel rituals
- physical room and event cards
- playful rules and warnings
- bold editorial/broadcast typography
- video-first atmosphere

## Why The Concept Worked

Late Check FM worked when the page behaved like a clear brand object instead of a fragile scroll experiment.

The winning direction was not generic hospitality UI. It was a loud, physical, authored hostel world with readable cards and a strong voice.

## What Future Agents Should Transfer

- Preserve strong concept energy before adding mechanics.
- Use physical card/sticker/label language only with text-fit and protected zones.
- Keep brand voice visible in headings, CTAs, and card copy.
- Use video as a hero-stage asset, then remove, dock, or posterize it.
- Treat mobile success as meaningful evidence, not as a secondary afterthought.

## What Future Agents Must Not Copy Blindly

- Do not reuse the exact visual style everywhere.
- Do not use giant typography if it clips at viewport edges.
- Do not use horizontal scroll unless active content is fully readable.
- Do not add floating stickers unless anchored and width-sweep tested.
- Do not preserve a cool motion idea after it harms layout integrity.
