# Longform Story Index Layout

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a long-form story page with a small top reading bar, a huge serif opening, a side index timeline, and a large body section. It turns a personal narrative into a structured reading interface.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: centered reading column with a persistent index rail.
- Hierarchy: opening salutation, lead paragraph, chapter title, body copy, and field note all have distinct levels.
- Spacing: generous vertical rhythm makes long content feel slow and readable.
- Typography: serif headings and body text create literary tone.
- Materials/colors: dark background with warm accent for active index state.
- Interaction/product pattern: scrollable narrative with chapter navigation/progress.
- Emotional tone: reflective, authored, and patient.

## What future agents should copy
- Give long-form content structure through an index, progress, and section anchors.
- Use small metadata to orient the reader without interrupting the story.
- Keep body copy columns readable and intentionally paced.
- Make the active section obvious but understated.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the biography, timeline, or personal story.
- Do not use long-form editorial UI when users need fast task execution.
- Do not make all product documentation look like a memoir.

## Best use cases
about page, long-form case study, founder story, editorial article, product manifesto
