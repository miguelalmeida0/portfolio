# Calm Frontend Manifesto Section

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a manifesto-style section with huge serif copy on the left and two stacked text panels on the right. The design uses a warm accent for key words and lets the copy carry the emotional weight.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: oversized statement paired with supporting proof panels.
- Hierarchy: headline dominates, then quote card, then paragraph card.
- Spacing: wide gutters and low density create calm reading.
- Typography: large serif display copy with restrained sans/mono labels.
- Materials/colors: dark surface, white card, black secondary panel, warm peach accent.
- Interaction/product pattern: approach/story section inside a portfolio.
- Emotional tone: thoughtful, human, deliberate, and quiet.

## What future agents should copy
- Use one large value statement and one or two proof panels instead of many feature cards.
- Let accent color highlight meaning, not decorate random words.
- Keep supporting cards text-driven and readable.
- Preserve breathing room around editorial copy.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the exact phrase or personal tone into unrelated products.
- Do not use huge manifesto text when the page needs task completion.
- Do not turn every supporting note into a floating card stack.

## Best use cases
portfolio, about page, product philosophy, founder profile, editorial landing section
