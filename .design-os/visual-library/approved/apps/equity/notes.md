# Equity

## Status
Approved app reference

## What Migi likes
- A true research terminal: dense data, source labels, ticker rows, and market state without generic dashboard fluff.
- Dark operational shell with one high-contrast accent used for active navigation and positive values.
- Tables, heatmaps, leaderboards, and headline feeds that support scanning and comparison.
- Recency/source/context labels that make financial claims feel traceable.

## What future agents should copy
- Use terminal density only when the user needs repeated scanning, comparison, and source-backed decisions.
- Keep search, filters, and active scopes visible near the data they affect.
- Use color semantically for gain/loss/status, not as decoration.
- Preserve table readability through sticky structure, clear columns, and disciplined row spacing.

## What future agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy tickers, prices, returns, news headlines, source names, or market claims.
- Do not use the dark financial-terminal style for products that are not data-heavy.
- Do not show live or sourced data unless provenance and recency are clear.
- Do not use red/green as the only state cue.

## Scope
Global for data terminals and app-specific for equity research semantics.
