# Market Command Center Overview

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a dark equity research overview with a left navigation rail, global search, market ticker strip, summary metrics, and a dense stocks table. It introduces the product as a command center for tracked securities.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: fixed sidebar, top search, ticker tape, metrics band, and table stack.
- Hierarchy: command-center headline and summary metrics orient before the dense table.
- Spacing: roomy section breaks keep the data-heavy screen readable.
- Typography: bold headings, compact labels, and numeric emphasis support scanning.
- Materials/colors: black/charcoal shell with bright green active accents and red/green financial values.
- Interaction/product pattern: search, filter ticker, add security, scan tracked rows.
- Emotional tone: precise, serious, and trader-terminal confident.

## What future agents should copy
- Put global search and market context at the top of financial tools.
- Use summary metrics to frame the data table below.
- Make active navigation unmistakable but not decorative.
- Keep row density high only when the columns are legible.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy financial values, ticker names, live labels, or source claims.
- Do not use this terminal layout for low-density consumer apps.
- Do not invent live market status without provenance.

financial terminal, research dashboard, market scanner, portfolio monitor, data-heavy operations
