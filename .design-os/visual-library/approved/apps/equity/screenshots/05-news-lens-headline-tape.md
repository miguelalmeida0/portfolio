# News Lens Headline Tape

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a news lens with headline counts, active sources, scope tabs, sentiment filters, ticker filters, a main headline tape, and right-side most-mentioned/breaking panels. It is built for scanning market news by source, ticker, and sentiment.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: filter column, headline stream, and right rail summary.
- Hierarchy: scope selection and headline count establish context before individual stories.
- Spacing: dense but well-separated lanes prevent the page from becoming a wall of text.
- Typography: bold headline cards with compact source and sentiment chips.
- Materials/colors: dark terminal surfaces with green active filters and red sentiment accents.
- Interaction/product pattern: refine by sentiment, ticker, source, and scope.
- Emotional tone: live, urgent, and research-focused.

## What future agents should copy
- Put filters in a stable rail when the stream is long and source-heavy.
- Show recency, source, tag, and sentiment close to each content item.
- Use side rails for summary signals that help prioritize.
- Label auto-refresh/live behavior explicitly.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy headlines, publishers, source counts, or sentiment labels as real data.
- Do not claim streaming or auto-refresh without implementation.
- Do not make news UIs dense without search/filter controls.
