# Mission Room Scenario Decision

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a mission room with a sidebar, searchable top bar, scenario cards, a selected fraud/refund case, primary action buttons, and a verdict card. It frames automation decisions as case-based work with evidence and owner context.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: scenario picker above a focused case detail and verdict summary.
- Hierarchy: selected scenario, decision title, primary mission action, and verdict are clear.
- Spacing: large work area offsets a dense navigation rail.
- Typography: enterprise sans with bold case titles and compact status labels.
- Materials/colors: off-white canvas, black sidebar, red/yellow/green state chips.
- Interaction/product pattern: choose scenario, run mission, review evidence, replay.
- Emotional tone: controlled, serious, and accountable.

## What future agents should copy
- Use concrete scenarios to make abstract automation governance understandable.
- Show verdict, owner, risk, and affected tool in one summary surface.
- Put the primary operation near evidence actions.
- Make selected cards visibly different from loadable cards.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy refund/fraud/legal content unless relevant.
- Do not add scenario cards without real scenario behavior.
- Do not copy the deep sidebar into simple products.
- Do not copy capture/crop artifacts around the screenshot.
