# Rewrite Lab Progress Trace

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a rewrite lab panel that explains a multi-step process: generating candidate rewrites, scoring candidates, selecting the strongest version, and building a trace. The active step has a progress indicator and supporting explanation.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: process trace sits beneath the output in a dedicated lab panel.
- Hierarchy: lab purpose, active progress, active step, then queued steps.
- Spacing: each step has enough separation to be readable.
- Typography: serif heading with compact process labels.
- Materials/colors: dark surface, blue progress, muted inactive steps.
- Interaction/product pattern: observe multi-step generation and scoring.
- Emotional tone: transparent, methodical, and trustworthy.

## What future agents should copy
- Explain multi-step AI or automation work with visible stages.
- Show which stage is active and which remain.
- Pair progress with a reason, not just a bar.
- Keep process detail optional or secondary to the main output.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not claim candidate scoring unless it actually exists.
- Do not show fake stages or fake progress.
- Do not overload simple workflows with unnecessary trace panels.

## Best use cases
AI workflow, process trace, generation pipeline, evaluation UI, automation transparency
