# Character-Led Rewrite Hero

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a dark landing hero for a rewrite tool, with huge serif copy on the left and a large illustrated writer character on the right. The CTA area offers a primary surprise action, a how-it-works action, and a case-study link.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: oversized product promise balanced by a memorable focal character.
- Hierarchy: headline, subtitle, CTAs, then illustration.
- Spacing: large empty black space makes the character and headline feel theatrical.
- Typography: serif display type gives the writing tool a literary identity.
- Materials/colors: black canvas, white type, pale blue accent, warm illustration.
- Interaction/product pattern: start with surprise, learn how it works, read proof.
- Emotional tone: cinematic, literary, humorous, and focused.

## What future agents should copy
- Use one strong focal character only when it clarifies the product metaphor.
- Let the hero explain the primary action in a single sentence.
- Keep secondary actions visually quieter than the primary CTA.
- Use case-study/proof links as secondary support, not the main event.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy Tolkien, the character art, or author-imitation claims.
- Do not use a big illustration if the app needs immediate operational density.
- Do not treat generated character art as a production asset without approval.
