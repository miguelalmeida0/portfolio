## Reusable Patterns

- Transformation tools need visible input, mode, process, output, and review.
- AI-like workflows should explain what is happening, not hide behind magic.
- Selected modes and scoring criteria should remain visible near the output.
- Character-led visuals can work when they reinforce the product metaphor.

## App-Specific-Only Patterns

- Author imitation, Tolkien naming, literary copy, and character art.
- Dark literary palette and serif-heavy styling.
- Any generated or illustrated assets.

## Top Approved Directions

1. Visible transformation loop.
2. Input/output workbench.
3. Process trace and scoring transparency.
4. Character-led creative identity.

## Warnings For Future Agents

- Do not make unsupported AI claims.
- Do not copy protected author/persona context blindly.
- Do not use character art without asset hygiene and approval.
