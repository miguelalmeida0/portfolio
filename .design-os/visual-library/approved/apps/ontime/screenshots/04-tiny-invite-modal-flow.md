# Tiny Invite Modal Flow

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a centered invite modal over a blurred plan grid. The modal contains the selected recipient, a short invite text field, category/time/place chips, recipient avatars, and a disabled or pending send action.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: modal isolates one social action while preserving the blurred background context.
- Hierarchy: recipient, invite text, send-to list, and bottom action are clearly ordered.
- Spacing: compact but comfortable modal with generous avatar spacing.
- Typography: friendly labels and direct helper copy reduce ambiguity.
- Materials/colors: cream modal, soft border, coral/yellow status accents, blurred backdrop.
- Interaction/product pattern: write invite, choose recipients, add options, send.
- Emotional tone: lightweight, reversible, and personal.

## What future agents should copy
- Keep modal flows focused on one decision.
- Preserve context with a dimmed/blurred background when it helps orientation.
- Show selected recipients visually before enabling send.
- Use disabled states honestly when required information is missing.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy friend identities or exact invite language.
- Do not blur important content if the modal depends on reading the background.
- Do not create a modal when the task needs full-screen focus or complex editing.

## Best use cases
invite modal, recipient selection, lightweight composer, social action, group messaging
