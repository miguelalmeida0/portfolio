## Visual summary
This reference shows a three-screen scooter rental flow with a dark operational shell, neon green accent system, a vehicle hero screen, a map availability screen, and a battery/status detail screen. It combines product object, location, and state telemetry without turning into a generic dashboard.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: three different states use the same shell while changing focal object, map, and gauge.
- Hierarchy: unlock button, scooter status, map pins, and battery percentage are instantly understandable.
- Spacing: centered panels and large touch controls make the system feel usable.
- Typography: small operational labels support the visuals without becoming noisy.
- Colors/materials: charcoal surfaces, green accents, shadows, and soft glow give the product a strong identity.
- Interaction/product pattern: find vehicle, unlock, inspect status, return to map.
- Emotional tone: futuristic, utility-driven, and confident.

## What future agents should copy
- Show the full operational loop with object, map, and status states.
- Use one accent color to mark live availability and action.
- Make the primary physical object feel real before adding telemetry.
- Keep status gauges large enough to be understood at a glance.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the scooter brand, exact map, or green accent by default.
- Do not fake real-time location, battery, distance, or unlock states.
- Do not overdo glow until controls look toy-like.
- Do not let map pins become decorative if there is no actual location interaction.
