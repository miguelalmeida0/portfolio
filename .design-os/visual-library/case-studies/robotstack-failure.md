# RobotStack Failure

RobotStack is a warning about product truth.

## What Failed

- Fake assets destroyed product truth.
- A UI shell without real content was not enough.
- Repeated images and fake robots were hard blockers.
- Product logic drifted before visual truth was established.

## Design OS Lesson

Do not let a visually promising shell hide broken product substance. For image-led or object-led products, assets must be truthful, varied, documented, and approved for production.

## Future Agent Rule

Before claiming done:

- validate the asset manifest
- check repeated primary-object assets
- confirm production assets are watermark/browser/editor free
- ensure the UI's visible object matches the product promise
- screenshot the real shell with real or clearly labeled placeholder content
