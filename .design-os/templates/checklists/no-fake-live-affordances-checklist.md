# No Fake Live Affordances Checklist

Use before final handoff when visible copy, badges, dots, feeds, or status labels imply live/realtime behavior.

## Search Terms

- `LIVE`
- live feed
- live lobby feed
- realtime
- real-time
- online now
- currently live
- streaming
- presence
- activity feed

## Required Proof If Found

- `data-live-justified="true"`
- `data-live-source`
- `data-update-cadence`
- `data-realtime-source`
- real timestamp/update behavior
- stale/offline fallback

## Blockers

- decorative `LIVE` badge with no live data
- fake live feed label
- fake pulsing live dot
- fake online/presence status
- fake activity ticker
- live terminology used as visual garnish

## Score Caps

- fake LIVE badge: max score 4
- fake live feed label: max score 3
- fake live icon/dot: max score 3
- fake realtime/presence status: max score 3
- no justification for live terminology: max score 5
- repeated fake activity language: max score 3
