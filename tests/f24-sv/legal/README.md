# Legal texts: read this first

These are drafts written to German and EU requirements as they stood in 2026. They are **not legal advice**. Every `[SQUARE BRACKET]` is a fact only you know. Fill each one in or delete the clause. Do not publish with brackets left in.

| File | Where it goes | Who needs it |
|---|---|---|
| `IMPRESSUM.md` | `/impressum`, linked from every page footer | The portfolio site (§ 5 DDG) |
| `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` | `/privacy`, linked from every page footer | The portfolio site (GDPR Art. 13) |
| `SECOND-VOICE-PRIVACY.md` | Inside the Second Voice app, linked at sign-in and in its footer | The live Second Voice app, a separate service |
| `FOOTER-NOTE.md` | Global footer of the portfolio | Every page |

Before publishing, check the following:

1. **Hosting provider:** confirm it and whether it keeps access logs, and for how long.
2. **Analytics and cookies:** confirm the site uses none. If it does, the privacy policy needs a section for each tool, and anything non-essential needs consent first.
3. **Ask MiguelLLM:** confirm which AI provider it calls, where that provider is based, and whether you store conversations.
4. **Fonts:** confirm they're self-hosted on every page. The Leu/Flow and F24/Second Voice handoffs already require this. Loading Google Fonts from Google's servers sends visitor IP addresses to Google, and a German court fined a site for exactly that (LG München I, 20 January 2022).
5. **Your employment contract:** check it for confidentiality or portfolio clauses that affect the F24 page.

A short review by a German IT lawyer, or a generator such as eRecht24 or the Datenschutz-Generator by Dr. Thomas Schwenke, is a sensible final check.
