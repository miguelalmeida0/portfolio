> Work for employers is shown with fictional mockups and simplified descriptions. No confidential information is published. Views are my own. Company and product names belong to their respective owners.

> [Impressum](/impressum) · [Privacy](/privacy)

Rules:
- Both links appear on every page, are reachable in at most one click, and are labelled exactly "Impressum" and "Privacy" (German law expects "Impressum" to be recognisable).
- Style: 13.5px, Muted `#506353`, links in Plum `#610D3D`, matching the site footer. Do not hide them behind a toggle.
