# Unsplash Asset Guidance

Use Unsplash as a real-image sourcing layer when image-first work needs credible photographic atmosphere, editorial texture, natural environments, spaces, people, materials, or product-adjacent context.

## Core Guidance

- Start from image roles, not random search.
- Query for subject plus mood, material, environment, or lighting.
- Match orientation to section composition.
- Match color filter to the selected palette only when it helps.
- Prefer images with strong focal structure and usable safe areas.
- Reject images that only satisfy "we need an image."

## Role Examples

- hero atmosphere: wide landscape image with negative space or strong scene.
- focal object: clear subject with readable silhouette.
- proof object: real-world object or material relevant to the concept.
- background atmosphere: low-detail field, texture, environment, or light.
- editorial fragment: cropped human/object/detail used as a design rhythm.
- texture/material: macro surface that supports palette and mood.

## Product Truth

Unsplash imagery can support mood, metaphor, and visual identity. It cannot prove actual product operation, real customers, facilities, internal labs, owned objects, or proprietary assets unless separately verified.

## Quality Checks

- Does the image support the section job?
- Is the crop robust at 1440 / 768 / 390?
- Can text be placed safely?
- Does the image fight the palette?
- Is source metadata preserved?
- Is alt text accurate?
- Is the image too generic, staged, or stock-like?
