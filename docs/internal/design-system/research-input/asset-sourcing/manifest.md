Main endpoints covered:

- `GET /search/photos`
- `GET /search/collections`
- `GET /collections`
- `GET /collections/:id`
- `GET /collections/:id/photos`
- `GET /topics`
- `GET /topics/:id_or_slug`
- `GET /topics/:id_or_slug/photos`
- stats endpoints

Why it matters for Miguel Design OS:

Unsplash sourcing gives image-first, landing, portfolio, creative, editorial, and product-concept work a path to real photographic atmosphere with source metadata, crop planning, palette relationship, alt text, and product-truth discipline. It prevents fake screenshots, placeholder rectangles, CSS-only asset theater, generic filler stock, and unsupported product claims.
