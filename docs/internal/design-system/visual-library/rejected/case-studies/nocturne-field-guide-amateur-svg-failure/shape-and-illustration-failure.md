# Shape And Illustration Failure

## Failure

The botanical illustration looked primitive and unprofessional: simple leaf blobs, unstable curves, arbitrary dots, and a stem/plate system without convincing botanical or editorial craft.

## Missing Requirements

- silhouette study
- reference grammar
- shape role map
- professional linework strategy
- detail budget
- material/texture strategy
- layer model
- text-safe zones
- responsive simplification plan

## Rule

Do not claim professional illustration when the art is primitive ellipses, generic leaves, or simple blobs. If botanical/character/object illustration is central, require a motif library, detail budget, linework strategy, and screenshot proof that the drawing does not look childlike.

## Score Caps

- primitive botanical or object illustration: max score 4
- shape system lacks role/layer/bounds/anchors: max score 5
- no reference grammar for illustration-heavy UI: max score 5
