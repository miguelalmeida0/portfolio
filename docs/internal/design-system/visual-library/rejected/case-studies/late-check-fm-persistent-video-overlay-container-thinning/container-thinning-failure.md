# Container Thinning Failure

## Bad Pattern

Cards were narrowed to preserve the video object, causing large titles and body copy to break awkwardly.

## Symptoms

- Room names split into fragments.
- Body copy becomes a skinny vertical column.
- Card proportions feel dictated by media avoidance instead of content.
- The page protects the video while damaging the information architecture.

## Rule

Never thin content containers to fit media. Media moves, docks, shrinks, exits, or stacks before text readability collapses.

## Minimum Expectation

- Major card titles wrap at phrase level, not letter chunks.
- Body copy keeps a natural line length.
- CTAs and labels keep content-aware inline size.
- Narrow viewports stack media above or below content instead of compressing cards.
