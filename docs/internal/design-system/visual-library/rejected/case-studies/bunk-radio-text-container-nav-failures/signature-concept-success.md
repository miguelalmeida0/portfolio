# Signature Concept Success

## Preserve

The concept direction is not rejected.

Preserve these ideas:

- Gen-Z hostel energy
- Bunk Radio as a memorable brand world
- video-first composition
- stickers, keycards, passport stamps, and hostel ephemera
- signature interaction direction
- rough, playful, social travel attitude

## Reject

Reject the execution pattern:

- stickers/cards covering critical text
- clipped words inside cards
- nav labels escaping controls
- huge empty space with no compositional purpose
- responsive behavior that collapses into dead space

Future agents should keep the Bunk Radio energy and rebuild the execution with layout integrity as a non-negotiable gate.
