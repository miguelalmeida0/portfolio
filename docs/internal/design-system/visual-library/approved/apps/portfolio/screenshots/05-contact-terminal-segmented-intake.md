# Contact Terminal Segmented Intake

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a contact section with three large contact cells followed by a terminal-like intake panel. The terminal asks why the visitor arrived and offers segmented choices such as recruiter, collaborator, curious, and lost.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: contact details sit above an interactive intake surface.
- Hierarchy: big question first, then selectable pathways, then typed prompt.
- Spacing: large card and terminal areas keep the playful interaction readable.
- Typography: serif heading contrasts with monospace terminal copy.
- Materials/colors: dark panels, thin borders, warm cursor/accent details.
- Interaction/product pattern: contact routing through selectable visitor intent.
- Emotional tone: personal, clever, and slightly playful without becoming loud.

## What future agents should copy
- Turn contact or onboarding into a clear intent-selection flow when it helps routing.
- Make selectable states visibly different from static labels.
- Use terminal styling only when typing or command metaphors are actually present.
- Keep playful interaction bounded inside a clear product surface.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy personal contact info or visitor labels directly.
- Do not use terminal UI only because it looks technical.
- Do not hide critical contact methods behind a novelty interaction.

## Best use cases
portfolio contact, onboarding intake, visitor routing, personal site, lightweight questionnaire
