# Portfolio

## Status
Approved app reference

## What Migi likes
- Editorial identity with a strong authored voice instead of generic portfolio cards.
- Large serif type, sparse navigation, and a single warm accent used with discipline.
- Project and contact sections that feel intentional, calm, and readable.
- Interactive flourishes, such as terminal-style intake, that support the personal story rather than becoming decoration.

## What future agents should copy
- Start identity-led sites with one unmistakable brand/person signal.
- Use typography, pacing, and whitespace as the main visual system when the content is authored.
- Let long-form story sections behave like editorial reading experiences.
- Make contact and navigation feel designed without stuffing the page with marketing sections.

## What future agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy Miguel's personal name, biography, contact details, exact portrait, or exact copy.
- Do not turn every app into a serif editorial site.
- Do not copy the dark palette unless the project independently needs an authored, intimate tone.
- Do not use terminal styling as a gimmick where it does not map to an interaction.

## Scope
App-specific with global lessons for editorial identity, pacing, and authored product storytelling.
