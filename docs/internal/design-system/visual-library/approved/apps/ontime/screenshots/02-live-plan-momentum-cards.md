# Live Plan Momentum Cards

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a live dinner plan card with arrival progress, participants, and an open-room action, followed by social momentum cards for forming or repeat plans. The screen makes social energy visible through status, avatars, and soft action language.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: live plan state sits above a row of momentum cards.
- Hierarchy: active plan and arrival progress are clearer than secondary suggestions.
- Spacing: cards are large enough for social metadata and action buttons.
- Typography: bold plan names and compact status rows keep scanning easy.
- Materials/colors: peach, cream, yellow, green, and blue cards separate plan types.
- Interaction/product pattern: open room, join softly, warm it up, run it back.
- Emotional tone: inviting, casual, and gently social.

## What future agents should copy
- Show live participation state with concrete progress, not vague badges.
- Use different card treatments for active, forming, and repeat plans.
- Let CTA language match the emotional risk of the action.
- Keep social proof close to the plan card.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy plan names, exact venues, participant initials, or live distances.
- Do not invent live progress if the product cannot support it.
- Do not use social-pressure language where the app should feel private or serious.
