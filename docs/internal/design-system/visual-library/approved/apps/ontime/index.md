## Reusable Patterns

- Friend or object context should precede social action.
- Social state should be visible and concrete: available, arriving, forming, watching.
- CTA wording should lower pressure when the interaction is socially risky.
- Repeated cards need both color and text labels for state.

## App-Specific-Only Patterns

- Exact names, faces, venues, plan copy, and availability claims.
- Warm peach/coral palette.
- The informal low-pressure voice.

## Top Approved Directions

1. Warm social composer.
2. Live-state plan cards.
3. Friend-first selection flow.
4. Focused invite modal.

## Warnings For Future Agents

- Do not fake social presence, distance, arrival, or availability.
- Do not copy capture/control artifacts as UI.
- Do not apply this playful tone to serious workflows.
