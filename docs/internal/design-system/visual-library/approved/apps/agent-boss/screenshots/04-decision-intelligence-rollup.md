# Decision Intelligence Rollup

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a decision-flow chart over the last 60 minutes and a compact rollup of capability labels such as proven, needs review, and revoked. It turns operational decision volume into a governance summary.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: large stacked bar visualization above status explanation cards.
- Hierarchy: chart first, then compact rollup of labels and proof/routing context.
- Spacing: chart occupies enough width to show flow over time.
- Typography: direct section titles and concise label descriptions.
- Materials/colors: white cards, green/amber/red state bars, dark sidebar.
- Interaction/product pattern: inspect rollup, understand state labels, trace decision quality.
- Emotional tone: analytical, governed, and legible.

## What future agents should copy
- Use rollups to explain state labels, not just count them.
- Keep status color semantics consistent across charts and cards.
- Pair high-level volume with qualitative status definitions.
- Give operational charts a readable time window.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy specific labels unless the product has the same governance model.
- Do not use stacked charts without explaining what each color means.
- Do not copy crop artifacts around the page.
