# Commerce Control Decision Map

## Status
Approved app reference

## Visual summary
The screen shows a commitment decision map with policy pressure on one axis and authority/evidence on another. Decision cards sit in allow, review, and block zones, followed by a next-decision explanation section.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: large decision map followed by a supporting explanation panel.
- Hierarchy: map title, legend, plotted cases, and next decision guide the user.
- Spacing: grid and cards have enough room to communicate position.
- Typography: clear labels and compact card metadata.
- Materials/colors: light canvas, subtle grid, red/amber/green decision semantics.
- Interaction/product pattern: inspect policy position, classify decision, review next step.
- Emotional tone: rational, governed, and transparent.

## What future agents should copy
- Use spatial maps when two decision dimensions matter.
- Label axes and zones directly inside the chart.
- Pair visual classification with a plain-language next decision.
- Use color and text together for decision state.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy commerce thresholds, refund amounts, or policy examples.
- Do not use a 2D map if the product has only one decision dimension.
- Do not imply authority/evidence scoring without real rules.
