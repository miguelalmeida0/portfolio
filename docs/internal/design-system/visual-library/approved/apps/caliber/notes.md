# Caliber

## Status
Approved app reference

## What Migi likes
- A writing/rewrite tool that makes the transformation loop visible: choose voice, tune mood, provide input, run, see progress, inspect output, compare candidates.
- Character-led art direction with a strong hero illustration before the product workbench appears.
- Dark literary interface with large serif type, restrained blue accent, and clear input/output panels.
- Honest processing and fallback states instead of vague AI magic.

## What future agents should copy
- For AI-like tools, show input, selected model/voice, transformation controls, generation state, output, and scoring trace.
- Use a strong visual metaphor only when it supports the product promise.
- Make intermediate states readable and meaningful, not decorative loading.
- Label fallback and scoring behavior clearly.

## What future agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy Tolkien, author names, literary claims, character art, or sample text.
- Do not claim real author imitation unless legally and product-wise appropriate.
- Do not copy the dark literary palette into unrelated tools.
- Do not use generated or illustrated character assets without asset approval.

## Scope
Global for visible transformation tools; app-specific for author-rewrite subject matter.
