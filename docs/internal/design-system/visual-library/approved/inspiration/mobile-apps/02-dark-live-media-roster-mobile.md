# Dark Live Media Roster Mobile

## Status
Approved inspiration

## Visual summary
This mobile reference focuses on a dark media roster screen with a compact brand header, horizontal profile avatars, large live content cards, and a translucent bottom navigation dock. It is a closer crop of the browsing experience, making the card rail and floating navigation relationship easier to study.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: stacked discovery rails make the screen feel like a browsable world rather than a flat feed.
- Hierarchy: live cards dominate, while avatars, badges, follower counts, and tabs remain secondary.
- Spacing: large rounded cards and soft gutters make dense imagery feel intentional.
- Typography: uppercase titles create strong poster-like impact.
- Colors/materials: deep black, saturated image thumbnails, orange live chips, and frosted controls build contrast.
- Interaction/product pattern: tap avatar or card, follow creator, switch tabs from a persistent dock.
- Emotional tone: premium, active, social, and visually collectible.

## What future agents should copy
- Build selection-first experiences with an obvious roster rail and an obvious hero/card rail.
- Let badges and stats prove state, but keep them smaller than the visual object.
- Use a floating dock only when it improves repeated navigation and does not hide core content.
- Combine image saturation with a restrained dark shell.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the casino wording, live-stream concept, or exact content images.
- Do not let the bottom dock cover important card actions.
- Do not make every product into a dark entertainment app.
- Do not use fake follower counts or live states unless the app can truthfully support them.
