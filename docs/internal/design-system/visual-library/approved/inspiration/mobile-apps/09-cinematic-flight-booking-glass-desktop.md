# Cinematic Flight Booking Glass Desktop

## Status
Approved inspiration

## Visual summary
This reference is a desktop or laptop travel booking interface rather than a mobile screen. It uses a cinematic Earth background, a large search-and-book headline, glass booking panels, destination cards, and travel category controls to make a functional booking form feel immersive.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: full-bleed atmospheric hero with the booking form anchored as the primary utility surface.
- Hierarchy: headline, category rail, destination cards, and form fields are distinct.
- Spacing: the form has enough internal padding to read as a premium control cluster.
- Typography: oversized clean display type creates strong travel intent.
- Colors/materials: deep blue, cyan glow, glass panels, and soft shadows give the layout cinematic depth.
- Interaction/product pattern: choose travel type, configure route, submit search.
- Emotional tone: expansive, futuristic, and travel-focused.

## What future agents should copy
- Put utility controls directly into the hero when the task is booking or configuration.
- Use background imagery to support product intent, not as empty atmosphere.
- Keep a small set of visible destination cards as proof of browsing.
- Let the form be beautiful but still clearly usable.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy device chrome, crypto/payment icons, destination imagery, or brand labels.
- Do not treat this as a mobile reference; it is useful for responsive hero and booking patterns.
- Do not add booking controls unless the app actually needs a form.
- Do not use glass panels over busy imagery without contrast checks.
