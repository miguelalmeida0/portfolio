## Visual summary
This mobile product reference uses a terracotta palette to frame a pendant lamp as the hero object. One screen presents the product image, short description, price, and add-to-bag action; the second screen shows technical details, material, light source, color swatches, and a product gallery.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: the object is centered and isolated, then repeated as a technical detail anchor.
- Hierarchy: product name, price, add-to-bag, material specs, swatches, and gallery have clear roles.
- Spacing: large object whitespace and rounded bottom panels make the product feel premium.
- Typography: compact labels and bold product naming create a catalog-like rhythm.
- Colors/materials: terracotta-on-terracotta creates a tactile material world that matches the object.
- Interaction/product pattern: product detail, variant swatches, gallery drawer, add-to-bag.
- Emotional tone: crafted, warm, architectural, and premium.

## What future agents should copy
- Make the product object the hero before adding commerce or specs.
- Use variant swatches as real controls with visible material or color meaning.
- Split emotional product story from technical details across adjacent states.
- Let the product material influence the UI palette when the product is physical and tactile.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the lamp design, product name, or exact price.
- Do not force a one-color palette onto products that need contrast or comparison.
- Do not show swatches unless variants exist.
- Do not use product-gallery thumbnails as decoration without a real gallery behavior.
