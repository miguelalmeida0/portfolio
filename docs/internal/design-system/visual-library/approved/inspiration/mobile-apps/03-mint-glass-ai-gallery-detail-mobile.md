## What future agents should copy
- Use frosted controls to keep creative tools present without overpowering the artwork.
- Separate gallery scanning from single-output inspection.
- Keep primary creation actions persistent and easy to reach.
- Let generated or preview imagery set the mood, but label creation state honestly.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy AI claims, brand labels, social counters, or creator names unless the product truly supports them.
- Do not reproduce the visible source/watermark artifact.
- Do not use generated imagery as a production asset unless it is explicitly approved and documented in an asset manifest.
- Do not let glassmorphism reduce contrast below readable levels.
