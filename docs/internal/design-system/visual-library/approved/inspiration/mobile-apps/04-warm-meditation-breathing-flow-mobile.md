# Warm Meditation Breathing Flow Mobile

## Status
Approved inspiration

## Visual summary
This reference presents a three-screen meditation or breathing flow with warm orange photography, rounded translucent controls, category chips, and player actions. The screens move from welcome/recommendation to selection to active breathing playback, giving the whole product a continuous emotional arc.

## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: each screen has one dominant calm image with controls arranged around it.
- Hierarchy: recommendation, session title, listen/play action, and progress controls are immediately legible.
- Spacing: generous vertical rhythm gives the wellness content room to breathe.
- Typography: soft, lightweight headline type matches the meditative tone.
- Colors/materials: warm orange overlays, translucent glass pills, and soft white buttons create a soothing system.
- Interaction/product pattern: browse category, choose session, play guided flow, adjust volume/theme.
- Emotional tone: intimate, calm, warm, and human.

## What future agents should copy
- Use a single emotional color world when the product is explicitly mood-led.
- Show a full state progression instead of only a static home screen.
- Pair content categories with one obvious primary action.
- Use translucent controls only where the background stays readable.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not make unrelated apps orange wellness products.
- Do not copy the portraits, meditation copy, or exact category names.
- Do not overuse low-contrast controls on bright imagery.
- Do not invent playback state if the app does not actually play or simulate sessions.
