## What Migi likely likes
- Composition: asymmetric dashboard panels create energy while preserving clear zones.
- Hierarchy: profile, security article, health score, login chart, and service cards each have a distinct visual weight.
- Spacing: large rounded cards and wide gutters keep the playful density under control.
- Typography: chunky labels and simple metrics feel approachable rather than corporate.
- Colors/materials: navy, lavender, coral, white, and soft chart colors create a memorable identity.
- Interaction/product pattern: read security tip, generate password, inspect health, review saved services.
- Emotional tone: friendly, secure, colorful, and product-specific.

## What future agents should copy
- Use illustration and color to make a technical/security topic feel less sterile.
- Give each panel a distinct job instead of repeating identical cards.
- Make score/gauge modules readable and tied to an obvious product promise.
- Balance playful surfaces with strong contrast and simple labels.

## What agents must not copy blindly
- Do not copy the password product name, user identity, or service logos.
- Do not add fake security scores, compromised counts, or reliability ratings.
- Do not reproduce the bottom-right overlay/crop artifact.
- Do not use playful cards if the task needs seriousness or precision.
