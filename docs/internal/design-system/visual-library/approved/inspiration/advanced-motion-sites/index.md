## Reusable Motion Lessons

1. Elite motion is designed in time, not sprinkled on a static page.
2. The navigation and menu are part of the choreography.
3. Media should reveal, crop, mask, or progress with intent.
4. Typography motion should set rhythm and hierarchy, not merely fade in.
5. Scroll progression should create a story arc.
6. A signature motion moment makes the page memorable.
7. Reduced motion must preserve meaning while removing movement.
8. Every section needs its own motion job.
9. Motion should clarify brand, state, or narrative.
10. One animation layer is not advanced motion.

## Do Not Copy

- Brand names, marks, copy, imagery, mascots, reels, or source content.
- Motion that depends on proprietary assets.
- Heavy animation where product speed matters more than cinematic feel.
- Effects without matching reduced-motion alternatives.
