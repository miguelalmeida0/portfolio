# Landing Artistic Benchmark

## Purpose

Tests whether landing, cinematic, artistic, and image-led pages behave like art-directed visual statements instead of generic marketing pages.

## What gold examples prove

- Composition: image-led first impression, poster logic, asymmetry, and a dominant visual thesis.
- Palette: expressive but readable color systems that feel authored.
- Typography: sparse copy, controlled type range, and strong editorial hierarchy.
- Image/object/media use: images behave as environment, focal object, or proof object, not decoration.
- Motion: if present, motion should support atmosphere, reveal, or section transition.
- Density: fewer objects with more impact; no giant empty AI furniture.
- Responsive behavior: emotional image-first experience survives tablet and mobile.
- Interaction quality: CTAs are clear without turning the page into a product dashboard.

## What rejected examples prove

- Blogspot/content-template structure is not artistic.
- Overlap, clipped text, and text escaping containers fail before taste scoring.
- Too much explanatory copy kills poster behavior.
- Fake assets or product-theater visuals destroy trust.

## Hard failure signs

- Generic SaaS or Blogspot structure.
- Decorative lined/grid backgrounds replacing art direction.
- Repeated feature rows.
- Fake screenshots used as product visuals.
- Text-heavy sections despite image-led references.
- Muddy generated-app palette with no exploration.
- Hero looks artistic but the rest becomes generic.

## Scoring criteria

- Image-led composition: 20
- Expressive balanced palette: 15
- Section rhythm and visual continuity: 15
- Sparse copy and editorial hierarchy: 15
- Image role discipline: 10
- Anti-AI-tell compliance: 10
- Responsive layout integrity: 10
- Screenshot evidence and done report: 5

## Signature Interaction Requirements

When award-level or signature interaction references are provided, landing work must choose one mechanic from `design-intelligence/signature-interaction-patterns.md` or justify why no signature interaction is appropriate.

Gold mechanics to consider:
- Kinetic Poster Board.
- Floating Product Ingredient Stage.
- Scroll-to-Assemble Cards.
- Editorial List Detail Modal.
- Portal Object Entry.
- Playful Entry Gate.
- Annotation Ritual.
- Specimen Lens Entry.
- Field Guide Plate Drawer.

Hard failures:
- generic section stack after signature references;
- decorative animation with no interaction story;
- copied reference branding instead of extracted mechanics;
- static SVG illustration where the selected mechanic requires animated reveal;
- browser-native SVG tooltip over an art-directed surface;
- annotation leader line crossing text;
- background texture reads as AI slop;
- no reduced-motion fallback.

## Elite Scroll Choreography Addendum

When scroll is central to this benchmark, also score against evaluation/benchmarks/elite-scroll-choreography.benchmark.md. Require a selected scroll pattern, route map, chapter map, mobile fallback, reduced-motion fallback, and post-build scroll-choreography-review. Fade-only sections, blank pinned frames, movement covering critical text, and horizontal routes without mobile fallback are hard failures.
