# Portfolio Experimental Benchmark

## Purpose

Tests whether portfolio, creative, experimental, and editorial interfaces build memorable identity and project navigation instead of generic portfolio grids or fake agency pages.

## What gold examples prove

- Composition: identity and navigation can be an experience, not just a grid.
- Palette: high-character color can work when it has contrast discipline.
- Typography: editorial structure, controlled scale, and restrained but memorable copy.
- Image/object/media use: a visual object or concept spine creates continuity.
- Motion: project reveals, page transitions, and hover states can carry identity when purposeful.
- Density: enough information to browse without burying the concept.
- Responsive behavior: the concept spine must survive mobile without becoming generic.
- Interaction quality: project selection, detail, and contact/intake paths remain obvious.

## What rejected examples prove

- Generic portfolio grids are not enough.
- Fake agency cliches weaken identity.
- Overlap, unreadable text, and prose-heavy sections block polish.
- Dogfood failures must feed back into Design OS only when system-level.

## Hard failure signs

- Generic portfolio grid.
- Fake agency copy or fake-perfect numbers.
- No visual object or concept spine.
- Art direction disappears after the hero.
- Palette is safe AI default with no exploration.
- Mobile reduces the project experience to a cramped list.

## Scoring criteria

- Strong identity: 20
- Project navigation as experience: 15
- Visual object or concept spine: 15
- High art direction with readable contrast: 15
- Restrained memorable copy: 10
- Motion/delight appropriateness: 10
- Responsive integrity: 10
- Screenshot evidence and done report: 5

## Signature Interaction Requirements

Portfolio and experimental work should consider signature mechanics from `design-intelligence/signature-interaction-patterns.md`, especially Portal Object Entry, Media Constellation Stage, Scroll Route Choreography, Directional Page Transition, Kinetic Poster Board, Annotation Ritual, Specimen Lens Entry, and Field Guide Plate Drawer.

Required proof:
- screenshot or motion evidence showing the mechanic;
- fallback route/list for accessibility;
- explanation of what was extracted and what was not copied.

Hard failures:
- generic section stack after signature references;
- static SVG illustration where a reveal mechanic is required;
- native browser tooltip over art-directed SVG;
- annotation lines crossing readable text.

## Elite Scroll Choreography Addendum

When scroll is central to this benchmark, also score against evaluation/benchmarks/elite-scroll-choreography.benchmark.md. Require a selected scroll pattern, route map, chapter map, mobile fallback, reduced-motion fallback, and post-build scroll-choreography-review. Fade-only sections, blank pinned frames, movement covering critical text, and horizontal routes without mobile fallback are hard failures.
