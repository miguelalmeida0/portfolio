## Rules

- Do not build a full page.
- Do not build navigation.
- Do not create extra sections.
- Do not use ambient background blobs, generic glows, dot fields, or decorative orbits.
- Do not use primitive ellipse/blob botanical, character, mascot, or product-object art.
- Stop after the isolated illustration review unless explicitly instructed to continue.

## Review Criteria

- reference grammar extracted
- silhouette believable
- linework strategy visible
- detail budget appropriate
- material/texture specific
- mobile crop survives
- accessibility/decorative status clear
- Migi approval or explicit pass recorded when required
