# No AI Theater Chrome Checklist

- No decorative `Narrative` chip.
- No decorative `Fictional incident` chip.
- No fake severity/status chip unless backed by product data.
- No `Replay` chrome unless there is an actual replay workflow.
- No `Mission` or `Mission Room` chrome unless it is a real domain concept.
- No oversized replay/status metadata strip.
- No fake `Case File`, `CL-####`, dossier, or operation ID.
- No repeated narrative/status chips across every page.
- All visible labels map to a user task, route, filter, state, or decision.
- Any allowed dramatic/domain label has documented product reason and data/state behavior.

## Score Caps

- fake narrative/fictional incident chip: max score 4
- fake severity/status badge used as theater: max score 4
- replay/mission chrome used as default identity: max score 4
- oversized replay metadata strip: max score 4
- fake case-file or dossier identifier: max score 3
- repeated AI-theater chips across pages: max score 3
